# Crowd Counter — LinkedIn cut

## Angle

A job-hunt showcase for recruiters, hiring managers and business owners: a pocket device that gives a live headcount estimate and turns green when the crowd hits a target. It is shown through its practical uses and ends on a contact card.

## Format

- Square, 1080×1080
- 30 fps, 22 s
- 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s); every cut lands on a beat
- Tone: default (punchy, clean), plain language

## Visual identity

## Storyboard

| Scene | Time (s) | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Hook | 0–3 | "Paid if 120 people show up." word by word, then "Who's counting?" |
| 2 Reveal | 3–6 | Device slides up, warming up. "Crowd Counter" — "A live headcount estimate that fits in your pocket." |
| 3 Glance | 6–10 | Count ticks up grey→amber. "One number. Turns green at your target." |
| 4 Payoff | 10–12.5 | Green ABOVE 120, chime. "Target hit. Settle up with your own number." |
| 5 Uses | 12.5–16.5 | Four cards: Bands, Venues, Events, Pop-ups. |
| 6 Trust | 16.5–18.5 | "Counts devices. Never identifies people." "An estimate, not a certified count." |

## Honesty notes

- On-screen counts are illustrative demo values, not measurements.
- No accuracy claims and no battery claims.
- Contact details are shown at the owner's request.
